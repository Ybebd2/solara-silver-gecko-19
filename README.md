An open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built for the
[$1,666 make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01) — a complete,
original, open-source browser game that runs offline from static files.

## Play

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

No build step, no network requests at runtime. Three.js r187 is vendored
under `game/vendor/three/`.

## The game

You are **Kai**, a fixer climbing from ROOKIE to FIXER across eight jobs
in the sun-soaked city of Solara — seven districts, from Marina Bay to
Neon Row to Solara Beach:

1. **LUPE — First Fix** — meet Lupe at MO'S on Neon Row
2. **SULLY — Tow Job** — drive a car to Dockside
3. **MARCO — Marco's Run** — a package to Old Port, no scratches
4. **BRANDT — The Ledger** — hold the Downtown office 5 seconds
5. **AURORA — Aurora's Light** — walk the beach boardwalk
6. **LUPE — Hot Wheels** — jack a fresh ride on Neon Row
7. **SULLY — Heat Wave** — lose the cops, reach Palm Heights
8. **BRANDT — The Solara Job** — the bank door, then the Marina getaway

Systems: on-foot and driving movement, traffic and 64 pedestrians,
carjacking, a 5-star wanted system with cruisers, officers, roadblocks
and a police helicopter whose searchlight beam is computed live from
the lamp under its nose, two-way dialogue, four radio stations
(NEON 101.5 included), crew abilities (Mara's Ghost, Sully's Pit Crew,
Kiki's Lookout), F9 autopilot, 20 collectible Golden Geckos, a HUD with
minimap, health/armour, cash and rank — all in bright 14:00 daylight.

## Controls

WASD move · mouse drag orbit · Shift sprint · Space jump · E mission ·
F enter/exit car · R reload · 1–5 weapons · Q radio · M map ·
F9 autopilot · Z/X/C crew · Esc pause · H hide HUD
